# Sacred Spaces: Life Partitioning & Privacy Model

Neev organizes modern family life into three **Sacred Spaces**, each with strict boundary semantics and permission models:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                   NEEV FAMILY OS                       │
├────────────────────┬──────────────────┬────────────────┤
│   Personal Space   │  Nuclear Space   │Family Sanctuary│
│   (Confidential)   │ (Home & Kids)    │(Multi-gen Root)│
├────────────────────┼──────────────────┼────────────────┤
│ • Career Switch    │ • School Exams   │ • Grandparents │
│ • Tech Interviews  │ • Homework Due   │ • Festive Puja │
│ • Private Goals    │ • Pediatrician   │ • Heirloom Cook│
│ • Deep Work Blocks │ • Home Chores    │ • Beloved Pets │
└────────────────────┴──────────────────┴────────────────┘
```

---

## 1. Personal Space (Private & Confidential)
- **Visibility**: Visible only to the authenticated individual.
- **Key Domains**:
  - **Career Switch Roadmap**: Staff/Lead Architect interview preparation, system design drills, and compensation benchmarking.
  - **Personal Deadlines**: Confidential work commitments, certifications, and focus blocks.
  - **Private Reflections**: Personal habits and private mantras.
- **Google Services Linked**:
  - Confidential Google Meet links for remote interview rounds.
  - Private Google Calendar focus blocks.

---

## 2. Nuclear Space (Immediate Household & Children)
- **Visibility**: Shared between partners, spouses, and children.
- **Key Domains**:
  - **Child School & Academics**: Science fair exhibitions, term exams, homework diary tracking, and school bus schedules.
  - **Pediatrician & Doctor Consultations**: Immunization record tracking, clinical appointments, and seasonal booster checklists.
  - **Office Work & Deliverables**: High-impact deliverables, sprint deadlines, and stakeholder presentations.
  - **Daily Household Chores**: Meal planning, grocery procurement, and school uniform pressing.

---

## 3. Family Sanctuary (Multigenerational Circle)
- **Visibility**: Full family circle, including grandparents, aunts, uncles, cousins, and beloved pets.
- **Key Domains**:
  - **Elder Wisdom & Oral History**: Grandparents' stories (e.g. Shantiniketan 1968, ancestral memoirs), audio recordings, and lifework reflections.
  - **Festivals & Pujas**: Auspicious muhurat timings, 51 diya lighting traditions, samagri procurement, and extended family RSVP.
  - **Heirloom Recipes**: Traditional delicacies (Chhanar Payesh, Nankhatai, seasonal mango chutney) passed through generations.
  - **Beloved Pet Care**: Simba's daily park frisbee walks, veterinarian vaccination logs, grooming routines, and wholesome homemade oat treats.
