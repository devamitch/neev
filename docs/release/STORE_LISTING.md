Neev is a private sanctuary operating system designed exclusively for families to stay connected, coordinated, and safe.

Built with an authentic Pista and Terracotta sanctuary interface, Neev provides an ad-free, sacred digital space where family members can share real-time location radar, plan meaningful functions and rituals, coordinate schedules, and preserve cherished memories in an encrypted heirloom vault.

🏡 PRIVATE FAMILY SPACES & SANCTUARY DASHBOARD

• Sanctuary Dashboard: Unified overview of daily family schedules, meal plans, and household reminders.
• Gentle Presence: Check in on family wellbeing with soft status indicators.

📍 SPATIAL 3D RADAR & EMERGENCY MAPS
• Live Family Radar: Real-time, peer-to-peer safe location awareness with offline tile support.
• Sacred Safe Zones: Automatic ambient alerts when family members safely arrive at home, school, or work.
• Emergency Broadcast: Immediate one-tap family beacon that relays emergency alerts even with limited connectivity.
