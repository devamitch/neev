Neev is a private sanctuary operating system designed exclusively for families to stay connected, coordinated, and safe.

Built with an authentic Pista and Terracotta sanctuary interface, Neev provides an ad-free, sacred digital space where family members can share real-time location radar, plan meaningful functions and rituals, coordinate schedules, and preserve cherished memories in an encrypted heirloom vault.

🏡 PRIVATE FAMILY SPACES & SANCTUARY DASHBOARD

🪔 FAMILY FUNCTIONS, RITUALS & CALENDAR
• Shared Event Scheduler: Coordinate family gatherings, celebrations, cultural rituals, and appointments.
• Memory Timelines: Collaborative family timeline chronicling milestones, anniversaries, and precious heirlooms.

🔒 HEIRLOOM KNOWLEDGE VAULT & ENCRYPTION
• Document Enclave: Safely store essential family certificates, medical records, and legal papers on device.
